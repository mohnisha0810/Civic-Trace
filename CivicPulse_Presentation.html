ALGORITHMIC CORE

The Solution to PS 9: Explainable Dynamic Priority Scoring (EDPS)

The mathematical multi-vector formulation driving intelligent, equitable civic triage.

THE CORE ALGORITHM
Priority Score = min(100,  w₁·Sᵥ + w₂·Z_w + w₃·Cᵥ + w₄·E_q + w₅·T_e)
Calibrated Weights: Visual Hazard (30%) • Zone Sensitivity (25%) • Cluster Velocity (20%) • Civic Equity (15%) • SLA Decay (10%)
DYNAMIC RANGE
0 — 100
● Sᵥ: VISUAL HAZARD & SEVERITY (30%)
Computer Vision depth & hazard grading. Flags exposed high-voltage wiring, potholes >15cm deep, or blocked 4-lane roads. Generates an immediate danger coefficient.
● Z_w: ZONE SENSITIVITY KERNEL (25%)
Geospatial distance-decay factor to critical infrastructure: <100m from Hospitals, Schools, Major Transit Intersections, or Flood-Prone drainage basins.
● Cᵥ: CLUSTER VELOCITY & CROWD ECHO (20%)
Spatial DBSCAN clustering (50m radius). Escalates exponentially if report velocity surges (e.g., 9 citizen upvotes in 2 hours = emergency pipe burst surge).
● E_q & T_e: EQUITY INDEX & SLA ESCALATION (25%)
E_q: Demographic normalization ensuring low-income wards aren't deprioritized due to smartphone disparity. T_e: Exponential SLA time-decay penalty.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Public Resolution Tracker & The "Proof-of-Fix" Protocol

Restoring democratic trust with verifiable Before/After computer vision and community consensus.

● STEPWISE TRACKING
5-Stage Public Milestone
1. Submitted: Geotagged ticket logged publicly.
2. AI Triaged: EDPS score assigned with public reason.
3. Dispatched: Assigned to ward contractor with SLA.
4. Work Submitted: Contractor uploads completion proof.
5. Verified & Closed: Citizen consensus validated.
● COMPUTER VISION AUDIT
Before / After AI Diff
Coordinates Lockdown: After-photo must be taken within 15 meters of original reported GPS coordinates.
Image Difference Analysis: CV model compares Before vs. After imagery to ensure defect is genuinely rectified.
Anti-Fraud Shield: Detects recycled stock photos, invalid lighting angles, or manipulated metadata.
● DECENTRALIZED TRUST
Community Consensus Gate
24-Hour Verification Window: Original reporter and 3 nearby residents receive an SMS/push confirmation prompt.
"Did They Fix It?" Vote: Citizens click "Yes, Verified" or "Still Broken / Poor Quality".
Automated Escalation: If rejected, ticket re-opens at Priority 95 with an administrative audit penalty flag.
